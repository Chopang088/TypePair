About TypePair

A typography tool for designers and developers — built to demonstrate Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics in a real, usable product. Every font is from Google Fonts: open-source and free for personal and commercial use.

How to use

  • Browse pairings on the Explore tab, or use the search bar to filter by style.
  • Click directly on any heading or body text to type your own content — changes sync across all cards.
  • Hit the heart ♥ to save a pairing to your collection.
  • Click </> on any card to reveal CSS + Google Fonts import, ready to copy.
  • Toggle dark mode 🌙 from the top-right corner.
